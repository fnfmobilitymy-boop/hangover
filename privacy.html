1. What this statement covers

This statement describes the current KENNY frontend demo. The identity and contact details of the organisation responsible for a live store will be added before commercial launch. It is not a claim that an operational retail backend or customer database exists.

2. Information you choose to provide

A demo profile can store your name and email locally if you consent. Product reviews store the name, rating and text you enter in this browser. Checkout and contact forms use your input only for the current demonstration; that input is not sent to a KENNY server or saved as order contact data.

3. Browser storage

Local storage may contain shopping-bag product IDs, sizes and quantities; favourites; language and region preferences; demo profile details; demo reviews; consent choices; and demo order references, item counts, totals and dates. Order records do not include your delivery address or payment details.

4. How data is used

This information supports the demo functions you select: restoring your bag, showing saved items, changing language, displaying your voluntary profile, saving demo reviews and looking up local demonstration orders. It is not used to send marketing messages or process purchases.

5. Cookies and optional preferences

The demo uses browser storage for essential functions and offers analytics and marketing preference controls. No advertising pixels or analytics SDKs are installed. Accepting optional preferences records a choice only; it does not activate a tracking service.

6. Hosting and external providers

Your browser requests website files from the hosting service. The host may process technical request data, such as an IP address and access logs, under its own practices. Google, Facebook, Apple, payment providers and social platforms are not connected to the demo forms. Any future integration will require an updated notice.

7. Retention and your choices

Local demo data stays in this browser until you remove it, clear site storage or use the available delete controls. You can remove bag items and favourites, clear a demo profile or clear all KENNY local data with the button below. Privacy preferences can be changed at any time from the footer.

8. Security and children

Do not enter sensitive personal information, payment-card numbers or passwords in this demo. Local browser storage is not a secure account system. Demo membership requires confirmation of age 18 or older; children’s catalogue items are intended to be viewed by an adult shopper.

9. Rights and future changes

Before real customer data is collected, KENNY will publish verified contact details, purposes, applicable legal requirements, retention arrangements, provider information and a process for privacy requests. Where applicable, access, correction, deletion and consent-withdrawal rights will be respected.
