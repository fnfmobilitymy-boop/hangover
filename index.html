FROM THE FACTORY. FOR YOUR EVERYDAY.

Every good shoe.
Starts with care.

Before there was a KENNY logo, there was a workbench. Sketches, material samples and half-finished pairs filled the space. Working close to the making taught us something simple: a great shoe is built from details most people never see.

We wanted to bring that care out of the workshop and into everyday life. So we imagined KENNY: a brand for the early train, the evening run and the unplanned detour. Shoes with character, made to make your next step feel like your own.

THE THINKING BEHIND EVERY PAIR

From an idea.
To your next step.

A closer look at the shoemaking journey.
The details make the difference.

01 / DESIGN

Start with the wearer.

A silhouette begins with a question: where will this pair take you? Shape, proportion and fit are considered together, before colour brings the idea to life.

02 / MATERIAL

Every layer has a purpose.

Texture, flexibility and cushioning each change how a shoe feels. The aim is a thoughtful combination that supports its intended use, from city walks to daily movement.

03 / DETAIL

Care in the small things.

The curve of a seam. The feel of a lining. The way a lace sits. These quiet details connect design to the experience of wearing a pair every day.
